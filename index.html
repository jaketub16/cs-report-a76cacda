Momentum: better or worse?

Getting a bit better, mainly because there's less email. Slow replies went from 16 in July to 6 in September and 6 in October, and the share fell from 43% to 32%. Client email volume also roughly halved (37 in July to 15–19 a month since September). The typical wait has stayed at 2–3 hours since August. Week to week it's patchy: most weeks have 0–2 slow replies, but there are spikes (Aug 31: 4 of 5 slow; Oct 19: 2 of 4, including TrustStar 63h). The bad weeks come in clusters on the same accounts (DBSync, HRG, TrustStar), which points to specific accounts being dropped rather than general overload.

Per person: load and speed

Does slowness rise with load? Partly. Shane's worst month (July: 11 slow) was also his busiest (25 client emails, 85 meetings, ~21 clients). But his September got slower (typical wait 10.6h) with only 8 emails, so it's not only volume. William was slowest in June (13h typical) and has been fast since August as his load dropped. Yona took over most of the email threads in October (49, more than Shane and William together). Her answer rate is still 100%, but 3 waits were long.

How this was counted, and what's missing

We read every client email found in Jake's inbox (210 total) and left out 88 that didn't need a reply: thank-yous, FYIs, emoji reactions, confirmations, prospects, the WANT Branding legal dispute after Jul 28, and Wayflyer (a lender). Replies from Jake count as answered, and so do documented calls and answers sent in another thread. The wait is the real time the client waited, nights and weekends included.
